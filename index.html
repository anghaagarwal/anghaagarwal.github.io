Research Interests

My research focuses on mathematical analysis, particularly fractional Sobolev spaces in Euclidean spaces and metric measure spaces. Current interests:

  • Fractional Sobolev spaces
  • Analysis on metric spaces
  • Besov and Triebel-Lizorkin spaces
  • Non-linear potential theory
